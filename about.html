About Agro Resources and Formulation Co.limited

Agro Resources and Formulation co.limited
is a poultry feed company dedicated to producing high-quality,
nutritious, and well-balanced chicken feed for farmers and poultry businesses.
our goal is to support healthy bird growth, improve productivity, and help poultry
farmers achieve better results.

We provide different types of poultry feed, including chick starter, grower feed,
finisher feed, and layer feed, formulated to meet the nutritional needs of chickens
at different stages of growth.

our mission

our mission is to provide reliable and affordable poultry feed while helping
farmers maintain healthy and productive birds.
